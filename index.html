      Questions partners ask first.

      Is there a cost to apply?

      No. Applying is free. Pricing depends on your program and volume, and your partner manager walks you through it on the call.

      Are there minimum orders?

      It depends on the program. White-label brands, clinic supply, and telehealth programs each work differently. We'll give you the exact numbers for your setup on the call.

      How does onboarding work?

      After you apply, a partner manager calls you to understand your business. If it's a fit, you get access to our private partner portal with the catalog, pricing, and onboarding steps, and we set up your program from there.

      What's the difference between RUO products and the clinical programs?

      RUO (research use only) products are sold for laboratory research and are not for human consumption. Telehealth and regenerative medicine are separate clinical programs delivered by licensed providers. They are run, sold, and labeled separately.

      Do I need a medical license?

      Not for an RUO brand or the affiliate program. Clinical programs have requirements that depend on your state and your role. We'll tell you what applies before you commit to anything.

      Will my customers see the Peplogix name?

      White-label means your brand goes on the product and the packaging. Your partner manager will confirm exactly how labeling and shipping work for your program.
